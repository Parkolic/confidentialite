Parkolic Privacy Policy

Parkolic Android app (package name com.parkolic.app).
Effective 11 November 2026.

In this policy, “Parkolic” means the app and its publisher.

The app is in French: app labels are quoted in French, followed by their meaning in English. This is a translation of the French version, which prevails in case of difference.

Summary

  • Parkolic works without an account and without a server.
  • Your credits, ratings, photos and settings stay on your phone. Parkolic has no access to them.
  • Your location is used in the moment. It is never sent and never stored, except when you tap “Je suis à l'entrée” (I'm at the entrance).
  • No ads, no audience measurement, no data sold.
  • To work, the app contacts a few outside services: updates (Expo), photos (Wikimedia Commons), the base map (OpenFreeMap) and wait times (Queue-Times.com). Details below.

1. Data controller

Parkolic is developed and published by Jordane Alexis BRUAT, an independent developer acting in a non-professional capacity, in France.

Contact for any question about your data: support@parkolic.fr.

2. Data stored on your phone

The app stores the following data in its own storage space, on your phone:

  • your rides and credits (date, time, attraction, “sur place” (on site) or “déclaré” (declared));
  • your ratings, your wish list and your day plans;
  • your catalogue corrections and your settings;
  • the photos and videos you attach to a ride (the app keeps a copy);
  • entrance locations recorded with “Je suis à l'entrée”;
  • the first name or nickname chosen in “Entre amis” (Among friends), and the profiles your friends send you.

The app sends this data to no one. Neither Parkolic nor any third-party service has access to it.

It stays on your phone until you delete it (see “Deleting your data”).

3. Permissions requested

Each permission is explained before it is requested. You can refuse it or withdraw it at any time in Android settings. The app keeps working without it: only the related features stop.

Location (precise or approximate)

It is used only while the app is open, to:

  • sort parks and attractions by distance;
  • know whether you are in a park, in order to show “Tu es au parc” (You're at the park) and suggest a credit near an attraction;
  • mark whether a credit was recorded “sur place” (on site). Only this yes or no is stored, not the location;
  • centre the map on you (“Autour de moi” (Around me), “Recentrer sur ma position” (Recentre on my location)).

Your location is never sent off the phone. It is stored in only one case: when you tap “Je suis à l'entrée” on an attraction's page. That location then becomes the attraction's entrance, on your phone only.

The app does not track your location in the background.

Camera

  • Take a photo or film a video for a ride.
  • Scan a friend's QR code. The image is analysed on the phone; it is neither stored nor sent.

Photos and videos

Pick photos and videos from your gallery to attach them to a ride. The app keeps a copy. It does not read the rest of your gallery.

A photo may contain information added by your phone, such as the date or place it was taken. This information stays in the copy kept by the app.

Microphone

Parkolic never records sound by itself. When you film a video from the app, your phone's camera app films it, with sound, and Parkolic keeps a copy.

4. Data sent off your phone

All these connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Like any internet connection, each one sends your phone's IP address to the service contacted.

App updates: Expo (United States)

When the app opens, then at most every 15 minutes when you come back to it, the app asks Expo's EAS Update service (650 Industries, Inc.) whether a new version exists.

The request contains:

  • an identifier drawn at random at installation, unrelated to your name, your Google account or your phone;
  • the versions of the app and of the installed update;
  • if a launch fails: the error text and the numbers of the updates involved.

It contains neither your location, nor your credits, nor your photos.

  • Purpose: deliver fixes and new versions, and roll back to a working version if an update fails.
  • Legal basis: Parkolic's legitimate interest in keeping the app working and safe (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Expo's role: processor, acting on behalf of Parkolic. Expo states that it does not use this data for advertising or combine it with other data.
  • Transfer outside the European Union: Expo states that it complies with the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Retention: Expo does not state how long these requests are kept. The text of a launch error is deleted after 3 days.

Expo's policy: expo.dev/privacy.

Park and attraction photos: Wikimedia Commons (United States)

When a page has a photo, the app downloads it from Wikimedia Commons (Wikimedia Foundation), then caches it to show it offline.

The request contains the photo's file name and the app's name (“Parkolic”, its version and Parkolic's contact address, as Wikimedia asks). No data about you.

The Wikimedia Foundation handles these requests under its own policy: foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy.

Base map: OpenFreeMap (Hungary)

The park plan and the Carte (Map) tab show an OpenFreeMap base map (Hyperknot Software Kft.), built from OpenStreetMap. To show it, the app downloads the map data for the area displayed on screen.

OpenFreeMap therefore receives the area viewed. With “Autour de moi”, the map is centred on you: that area then roughly matches where you are. Your exact location is not sent.

According to its policy, OpenFreeMap does not keep IP addresses in its regular logs. Its error logs may contain the IP address and the request; they are deleted after 7 days. Policy: openfreemap.org/privacy.

Wait times: Queue-Times.com

For parks covered by Queue-Times.com (Queue Times Limited), the park page shows live wait times. While the page is open, the app requests that park's wait times, at most every 4 minutes. Once a week, it also downloads the list of covered parks.

The request contains only the park's number at Queue-Times. No data about you.

You can turn wait times off in the “⋯” menu of a park page. The setting applies to all parks: the app then no longer contacts Queue-Times. Queue-Times' policy does not cover its API: queue-times.com/pages/privacy.

Fallback map: OpenStreetMap (United Kingdom)

On the rare phones that cannot show the OpenFreeMap map, the app uses a fallback map, downloaded from the OpenStreetMap Foundation's servers. These also receive the area displayed. Policy: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy.

QR code reading: ML Kit (Google)

QR code scanning uses ML Kit, a Google tool. The QR code is read on the phone: its content is not sent. ML Kit sends Google technical information (phone model, Android version, tool performance), for diagnostics and usage statistics of the tool.

Links to other sites

Some links open your browser (a photo's page on Wikimedia Commons, licences, sources, Queue-Times.com, this policy). The site opened then applies its own policy.

5. Data you share yourself

No data is shared without action on your part. When you choose to share, you also choose the receiving app (WhatsApp, e-mail, Drive…). Its policy then applies.

  • Images and texts (credit, trophy, statistics, day summary, year in review): what appears in the image or the text.
  • “Entre amis” profile (QR code or message): your first name or nickname, your key figures and the list of your credits (catalogue identifiers). Nothing else.
  • CSV export: your rides, with their dates, ratings and the “sur place” mark.
  • Full backup (ZIP file): all the app's data, including photos, videos and recorded entrance locations. This file is not encrypted: keep it somewhere safe.

6. Android automatic backup

If Google backup is turned on on your phone, Android may copy the app's data to your Google account, in a private space. This backup is managed by Google, in your account, not by Parkolic. You can turn it off in Android settings.

7. What Parkolic does not do

  • No account, no server: Parkolic receives none of your data.
  • No ads, no advertising identifier.
  • No audience measurement and no tracking of your use.
  • No sale or rental of data.
  • No access to your contacts or your other apps.

8. Deleting your data

All data is on your phone, so it is deleted from your phone:

  • a ride: from the attraction's page;
  • a photo or video: by opening it full screen from the attraction's page;
  • a friend's profile: “Retirer … de mes amis” (Remove … from my friends), on their page;
  • all data: Android Settings › Apps › Parkolic › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app.

Clearing the data or uninstalling the app also deletes the random identifier used for updates. A new installation generates a new one, unrelated to the old one.

To be able to restore everything later, make a full backup first.

9. Security

  • The app's data is kept in the space reserved for Parkolic on your phone. Android prevents other apps from accessing it.
  • All the app's connections use HTTPS.
  • Protect your phone with a code or fingerprint: it is the best protection for your data.

10. Children

Parkolic asks for no age, full name or e-mail address. The only name entered is the first name or nickname in “Entre amis”, which is optional and only leaves the phone if the user shares it. If your child uses the app, help them choose who they share their profile with.

11. Your rights

The GDPR gives you the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability regarding your data.

  • Most of your data is on your phone: you can view it, correct it, export it (CSV, full backup) and delete it yourself.
  • For data processed by Expo for updates, write to support@parkolic.fr. Parkolic does not know your random identifier and cannot link it to you: clearing the app's data remains the surest way to break that link.
  • For any other question or request: support@parkolic.fr. You will get an answer within one month at most.

If you write to this address, your e-mail is used only to answer you. Exchanges are deleted 12 months after the last message.

You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority: cnil.fr/fr/plaintes.

12. Changes

If the app changes the way it handles your data, this page is updated before the new version, with a new date at the top.
